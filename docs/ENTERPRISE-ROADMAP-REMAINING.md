# TARAZPAD Remaining Enterprise Delivery Roadmap

## Phase 1.2 — Treasury, Distribution and Commission
- Accounting-backed receipts/payments
- Bank/POS reconciliation lifecycle
- Payment proposal and cash forecast
- Cheque lifecycle
- Trip/POD/delivery/return/cold-chain/settlement
- Collection handover to treasury
- Commission based on posted invoices, collections, gross profit, targets and clawback

## Phase 1.3 — AR/AP, Budget, Fixed Assets, Costing and Projects
- Aging, credit/debit notes, settlement center
- Budget controls
- Fixed asset lifecycle/depreciation
- landed cost and management costing
- project/contract accounting

## Phase 1.4 — CRM, Loyalty, BPM and Document Management
- CRM 360, pipeline, follow-up, SLA, tickets
- customer loyalty/wallet/liability
- forms/workflows/cases/correspondence/meetings/DMS

## Phase 1.5 — BI, Security, Consolidation and Operations Hardening
- semantic BI/report builder/KPI lineage
- SoD, field masking and export controls
- multi-currency/intercompany/consolidation readiness
- backup/recovery center, observability and large-volume performance gates

No phase is considered production-complete without database/API/UI/permissions/audit/accounting integration where relevant and passing E2E gates.
